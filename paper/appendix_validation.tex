\section{Extended validation tables}
All numbers in this appendix are read directly from the committed results
files cited in each caption; nothing is re-estimated for the manuscript.

\subsection{Multiplex discovery statistics (Tool 6)}
From \texttt{results/realguide\_risk.json}: 4{,}692 real guides from
crisprSQL; strong self-fold rate $8.95\%$ (mean self-fold MFE $-1.87$
kcal/mol); GC-window violation rate $13.4\%$ ($9.0\%$ high-GC, $4.3\%$
low-GC). The named discovery --- same-gene duplex enrichment --- flags
$22.50\%$ of 30{,}000 same-gene guide pairs vs $15.87\%$ of 30{,}000
cross-gene pairs and $10.81\%$ of random-spacer controls (two-proportion
$z \approx 19.9$). Same-gene pairs share exonic sequence context, which
raises antiparallel complementarity between guides; the effect survives
the random-spacer control, so it is not a base-composition artifact.

\subsection{Cross-species transfer ladder (Tool 10)}
From \texttt{results/beat\_azimuth\_v1.json}--\texttt{v7.json}, mean
per-gene Spearman on V1 mouse (9 genes), train RES human:
ridge $0.268$ (v1), GNN $0.218$ (v2), GBT $0.480$ (v3), rank-ensemble
$0.474$ (v4), deep-stack tuned $0.370$ (v5, flawed), leak-free OOF stack
$0.490$ (v6), multi-assay RES+V2 $0.547$ (v7). Bar: same-split Rule Set 2
replication $0.569$. The bar stands; v6 is our best honest challenger.

\subsection{Off-target replication summary (crisprSQL)}
From \texttt{results/offtarget\_scorers.json} and
\texttt{results/offtarget\_epigenetics.json} (LOSO mean AUC, 7 evaluable
studies): Hsu-style $0.556$, CFD-style $0.582$, CFD-style + epigenetic

a small negative, preserved.

\subsection{Large-deletion panel (Tool 8)}
From \texttt{results/riskflag\_panel.json}: 111 RefSeq accessions, 478 NGG
cut sites scored ($57.5\%$ LOW, $34.7\%$ MODERATE, $7.7\%$ HIGH), mean
locus risk $0.221$. Highest mean-risk loci: VHL $0.655$, Pik3ca $0.633$,
B2M $0.613$, Tet2 $0.526$, ALB $0.455$; highest single-site risk:
Pik3ca NM\_008839 $0.780$, Tet2 NM\_001346736 $0.763$, PCSK9
NM\_001407243 $0.715$. The score is a within-locus ranking device; no
cross-genome calibration is claimed.
