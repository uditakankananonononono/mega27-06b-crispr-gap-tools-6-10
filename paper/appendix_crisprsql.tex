\subsection{crisprSQL per-study summary}
\label{app:crisprsql-perstudy}
All 16 constituent studies of the crisprSQL off-target corpus used in the
Tool 10 replications, machine-extracted from
\texttt{results/crisprsql\_perstudy.json}. Cleavage is the study's own
validated off-target readout; the two right-hand columns are Spearman
correlations of guide GC content and guide--target duplex thermodynamic
stability against that readout within each study - both near zero in
almost every study, which is why the sequence-mismatch scorers (not
composition priors) carry the replicated signal.
